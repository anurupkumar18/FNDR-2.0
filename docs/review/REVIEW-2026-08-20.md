# FNDR v2 Plan Review: synthesis and action plan

Date: 2026-08-20. Method: four independent fresh-context review agents, each attacking one axis with no stake in the plan looking good, plus a synthesis pre-mortem. Full reviewer reports preserved beside this file:

- [feasibility-red-team.md](feasibility-red-team.md): capacity math, critical path, sequencing (15 findings)
- [coherence-audit.md](coherence-audit.md): cross-document defects, verified programmatically over all 102 tickets (40+ defects)
- [technical-verification.md](technical-verification.md): the eight load-bearing technology bets re-verified against primary sources (2 refinements, 4 partly-confirmed, rest confirmed)
- [product-devils-advocate.md](product-devils-advocate.md): product thesis attacks within the locked constraints (10 objections)

## Overall verdict

The plan's direction survives review: the stack triangle (Tauri + Rust engine, SQLite + Lance, llama.cpp + Apple frameworks) is confirmed against primary sources, the MCP-spine thesis holds, and the invariants are the right ones. What does not survive is the plan's own arithmetic and several credibility-critical details. Four systemic weaknesses, in order of danger:

1. **The schedule is not executable as cut.** Month 1 assigns the backend person 2 to 3x their capacity, the month-3 gate has zero slack while month 3 carries nine tickets the gate does not need, and the loads whipsaw (backend 20-3-9-2-0-0 across milestones). The plan was cut by feature phase, never checked against person-capacity.
2. **The quality program has credibility holes that defeat its purpose.** The bench tunes on its own test set (no frozen held-out split), nothing measures verdict faithfulness or answer overclaiming, the cold-start problem is encoded as a demo precondition instead of solved, and the daily payoff loop that retention depends on arrives in month 5.
3. **Three technical details are wrong or unproven where errors are most expensive.** The always-on embedder's cited Q4 GGUF does not exist (Q8_0, 639 MB, is the real artifact), the 768d contract silently requires app-side truncate-and-renormalize, per-PR real-model CI on macOS runners is not honestly possible (no Metal, 3 vCPUs), and the chosen ScreenCaptureKit crate has the exact failure history (leaks, stalled callbacks) that kills always-on apps.
4. **Consistency debt is already accruing.** The 08-20 revision updated the PRD and tickets but never amended the ADRs, producing 40+ cross-document defects: an unsatisfiable P0 (P0.5 requires authenticated companion endpoints while zero companion code ships), three different MCP tool counts, ten scheduling contradictions, and P0 numbers that appear in no acceptance criterion.

## Theme summaries

### 1. Schedule and capacity (full detail: feasibility-red-team.md)

The census: 38 of 102 tickets in M1, 20 of them on one backend person; ml has 13 in M2 including the entire retrieval core plus the eval program meant to gate it; frontend is starved in M1 (three of four tickets dep-blocked) then slammed in M2; platform idles in M4/M5. The gate's critical path is eight-plus serial P0 tickets crossing three lanes with the only buffer (month 6) sitting after the gate. Months 4 and 5 land on the December/January holidays, exactly where the plan schedules its only four-lane integration (meetings). Estimation was deliberately omitted, which means milestone assignment was an unvalidated implicit estimate.

Also: eval-first discipline structurally cannot hold in month 2 (the bench gate lands with or after the ranking code it gates); the first real agent touches the MCP surface in the gate month itself; T-205's acceptance criterion (byte-equal rebuild) is unsatisfiable with ANN index training; dogfooding-by-M2 is a hidden dependency with no dev-install ticket; and no ticket covers per-builder environment bootstrap, the classic first-two-weeks sink.

### 2. Product credibility (full detail: product-devils-advocate.md)

The strongest objections: cold start is unsolved (no sample vault, no backfill importers, no empty-vault state; connect-your-agent wires an agent to a vault that honestly answers NotEnoughEvidence to everything); no daily payoff loop before M5 while retention is measured by self-report; the bench grades its own homework; citations-resolve is the wrong target (resolution is not support, and no metric scores the Grounded/Partial/NotEnoughEvidence verdict itself); the month-3 demo script outsources its wow to the agent's prose (fix: the counterfactual cut, FNDR-off vs FNDR-on, and the live privacy negative demo); long-horizon ownership is unplanned (no backup/restore/export, re-embedding migration designed out by "born on the final contract", retention at P1, no multi-Mac stance); meetings consent is one line of UI copy against all-party-consent jurisdictions; the plan produces design docs but not the failure-narrative artifacts staff interviews run on; the 3D graph is the worst value-to-effort area and lands in the highest-leverage month; and the scariest macOS permission gets a paragraph of copy instead of a verifiable trust moment.

### 3. Technical bets (full detail: technical-verification.md)

Confirmed: LanceDB Rust crate (FTS/hybrid/RRF/reranker trait all real in Rust, v0.37.x), llama-cpp-2 embeddings and reranking, FluidAudio scoping and licenses (inference-only, exactly as planned; CC-BY attribution required), the dual-store architecture (field evidence validates both the failure mode and the chosen counter-pattern), rmcp on the 2026-07-28 spec.

Corrections to apply: ADR-003's embedder row cites a Q4 GGUF that does not exist (use official Q8_0, 639 MB; avoid community sub-8-bit quants); the reranker's trustworthy GGUF is the ggml-org conversion, not an official Qwen artifact; 768d requires app-side truncation and renormalization written into the contract and tests, plus a 768-vs-1024 ablation; do not serve embedding and reranking from one llama.cpp context.

Risks to schedule: the screencapturekit crate is a single-maintainer bet both shipped comparables declined (prefer periodic SCScreenshotManager captures over a persistent stream, pin exactly, soak-test in month 1, name objc2-screen-capture-kit or vendoring as fallback); tauri-specta is permanently release-candidate (pin exact `=rc` versions, define the i64/BigInt convention in fndr-types first); Qwen3-VL through the mtmd bindings is plausible but unproven (spike it).

### 4. Consistency debt (full detail: coherence-audit.md)

The five worth fixing first: P0.5 unsatisfiable (companion auth with no companion code); the MCP tool contract has three counts and two unratified tools (session_story, answer) with T-709 shipping one from the wrong lane without the four required tool artifacts; auth-always planned as auth-mostly; ten scheduling contradictions across PRD phases, epic headings, and ticket milestones (context packs are month 2 or month 3 depending on the document); and P0/goal numbers absent from every acceptance criterion (the 15-minute install, CPU under 5%, pack p95 under 2 s, the 80% usefulness target, the 2-minute legibility target). Also notable: the PRD's 400 MB idle budget exceeds the range ADR-001 calls indefensible when rejecting Electron, "telemetry" means opposite things in two documents, and the 08-20 revision left every ADR and two document status dates stale. Root cause: no rule that PRD/ticket changes amend the ADRs they touch.

### 5. Governance and pre-mortem additions (synthesis, not from the agents)

- **No decision authority is defined.** Four equal lanes, contract-first rules, and no named tie-breaker or product DRI. The founder-review workflow assumes someone owns the outcome. Fix: name a product DRI and a per-area technical DRI in the PRD team section; decisions stall otherwise.
- **No working agreements.** Cadence (weekly sync, demo Friday), review SLA (24h for cross-lane contract PRs), and hours-per-week assumptions belong in the roadmap header; the red-team's capacity math cannot be maintained without them.
- **Walking skeleton before the M1 buildout.** Reviewers converged on spikes; go one further: a deliberately ugly end-to-end slice (capture one screen, OCR, store, search it, serve one MCP tool) by week 3, before the full storage/index buildout. It de-risks the three spikes in one artifact and gives the team a running system to dogfood from week 3, which is also the earliest honest test of pain point 1 (context usefulness).
- **Hosting split is unresolved.** The POC lives on GitHub (releases, updater manifest) while work tracking is a GitLab board; T-903's updater and T-102's CI assume one home. Decide the repo host before bootstrap; the tickets.csv import format assumed GitLab while the release ADR assumes GitHub conventions.
- **Purge-and-restart policy for early dogfood data** (red-team found the exposure; the policy needs stating): stores created before T-803 lands are deleted, not migrated.

## What we got right (for balance)

The stack triangle held up under adversarial re-verification; the port/discard inventory was not challenged on substance by any reviewer; the invariants were judged the right ones (the findings are about sequencing and enforceability, not direction); the two v1 security regression tests are consistently named everywhere; and the tickets.csv transfer was verified defect-free across all 102 rows.
